    About this guide

    Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Guide text is written for this entry, in our own words, and each trophy names its sources beside the guide. Where a trigger is not yet pinned down by any source, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing. Counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting range challenges, 14 drone race locations and the rest) come from the PS5 trophy tracker on PSNProfiles, confirmed by GamesRadar and PowerPyx. Difficulty and time figures are our own estimates, based on those counts and on the PowerPyx (about 200 hours) and Push Square (250+ hours) platinum estimates.

    All 55 trophies are single-player; there are no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies and stop the game from saving, so keep them off your platinum run (PowerPyx, Push Square). This guide is not affiliated with Rockstar Games and uses no Rockstar logo or official artwork.

    Main sources: SixShore trophy guide (citing PowerPyx, Push Square, PSNProfiles, TrueAchievements, GamesRadar) · HUDWIRE mission list · GTA Vibes trophy list · GameMapa trophy list
